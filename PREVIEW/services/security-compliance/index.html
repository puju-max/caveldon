Security & governance · regulated workloads

Cloud engineering for sensitive and regulated systems.

Regulated buyers review vendors harder than anyone. On top of SOC 2 they ask about HIPAA safeguards, card-data scope or operational resilience. We build those controls into your AWS platform and make them provable, drawing on hands-on experience in healthcare and financial services.

What we do

Design and implement the technical controls, collect the evidence, fix the gaps, and work alongside your auditor or compliance platform.

What we don’t do

Issue audit opinions, certifications or legal advice. Those belong to independent auditors and lawyers, and anyone claiming otherwise is a red flag.

What we engineer

Six technical control areas worth evaluating.

Security controls support risk reduction. Documentation and evidence can support compliance assessments, but neither engineering nor automation establishes compliance on its own.

01 · ACCESS

Identity & privilege

Role boundaries, least privilege, MFA and single sign-on, service identities and regular access reviews.

02 · DATA

Data protection

Encryption, secrets, backup scope, data flows and region-aware decisions about where data lives.

03 · EVIDENCE

Logging & traceability

Change history, configuration records and audit trails, retained and easy to produce.

04 · POLICY

Guardrails

Account and subscription structure, policy-as-code, tagging and configuration rules that prevent drift.

05 · RESILIENCE

Recovery

Tested backups, recovery targets, alerting and a clear owner for each operational risk.

06 · REMEDIATION

Findings to fixes

Turn gaps into ranked tasks, implement them, and record what changed as evidence.

How it runs

From findings to evidence in four steps.

It usually starts with the Cloud Readiness Review, extended to your customers’ requirements. That covers the first two steps.

  1. 01 · SCOPE

    Map what’s in scope

    Systems, data flows, owners and which requirements actually apply.

  2. 02 · REVIEW

    Check the controls

    Access, logging, configuration, backups, and the gaps in evidence.

  3. 03 · FIX

    Close the gaps

    Ranked, reviewed changes, plus guardrails so problems don’t come back.

  4. 04 · PROVE

    Record the evidence

    Test the controls, collect the proof, and list any risk you’ve chosen to accept.
