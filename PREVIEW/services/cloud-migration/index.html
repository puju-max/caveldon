Our method

Five steps to a responsible transition.

We agree on scope and acceptance criteria before work begins.

  1. 01

    Discover

    Map dependencies, data, security and operational ownership.

  2. 02

    Design

    Choose destination, migration sequence and acceptance criteria.

  3. 03

    Prepare

    Back up, review access and rehearse where feasible.

  4. 04

    Move

    Perform a controlled cutover with monitored rollback decisions.

  5. 05

    Validate

    Check integrity, behavior, recovery and handover.

What you could receive

  • Discovery notes and dependency map
  • Target architecture and decisions
  • Cutover, backup and rollback plan
  • Validation results for work actually executed
  • Operational handover documentation
