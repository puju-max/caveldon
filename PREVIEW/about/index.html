How we work

Four promises we hold ourselves to.

The founding idea is simple: technology should create confidence, not complexity.

01 · CLARITY

Understand before changing

Every recommendation explains the problem, the alternatives and the likely consequences.

02 · DISCIPLINE

Build what can be run

Reviewed changes, useful documentation, real monitoring and sensible safeguards.

03 · HONESTY

Evidence over adjectives

We say what’s been done, what’s still an assumption and what hasn’t been measured.

04 · OWNERSHIP

Yours to keep

You own the code, the docs and the access. No lock-in, and a proper handover.

The name

Why “CAVELDON”?

It’s a coined name, chosen to stand for confidence, discipline and lasting progress. The meaning comes from the standards we hold, not from an invented history. The C— mark is a line drawing, like the diagrams we use to explain systems.
