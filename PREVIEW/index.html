Questions

Common questions before starting.

Have a question that is not covered? The Contact page is a simple place to start.

Do we need to know exactly what we need?

No. Most people come with a symptom: “deploys are scary”, “the AWS bill doubled”, “an investor asked about security”, “a customer sent a questionnaire”. Working out the cause is our job.

We’re an early-stage startup. Is this overkill?

No. Setting up the cloud properly at the start costs far less than fixing it after you grow. For early teams we keep it lean: the essentials for security, deployments and cost, and nothing you won’t use yet. If you have AWS startup credits, we help you spend them on the right things.

Can you be our DevOps team?

Yes. Most startups can’t justify a full-time DevOps or cloud engineer early on. Our monthly plan gives you a set amount of senior engineering time each month for improvements, releases, reviews and help when something breaks. When you’re ready to hire, we help you hire and hand over.

How much does it cost?

The first call is free. The Cloud Readiness Review is a fixed price, agreed in writing before we start, based on the size of your setup.

Projects are quoted at a fixed price and the monthly DevOps plan is a fixed monthly fee, so there are no open-ended hourly bills.

Who will actually work on our systems?

The engineer who scopes your project stays on it and does the hands-on work. If another engineer joins, you meet them first. The founder oversees every engagement. Access uses dedicated roles you can revoke at any time, and it is removed when the work ends.

Will you need access to our AWS account?

For the review, read-only access is enough. You create a dedicated role for us and can remove it at any time. We never ask for root credentials, passwords or customer data, and we’re happy to sign your NDA first.

Can you make us SOC 2 certified?

There’s no such thing as a SOC 2 certificate. A licensed CPA firm examines your controls and issues a report. We do the engineering: put the controls in place in AWS and your pipelines, and prepare the evidence the auditor will sample.

Do you work in our time zone?

Calls are scheduled at times that suit you.
